# candence 🕯️

*Warm clarity — attention that is fully-clear and fully-warm at once; the fusion of rigor and kindness that modernity treats as opposed.*

candence is built from Latin **candor** and its adjectival **candidus** — not the cold sterile white of modern usage but the *warm* white: the whiteness of a flame, a lamb's fleece, a dawn, a friend's face, the precise that was also sincere and kind before later ages pried clarity from warmth. To this the cathedral welds Latin **-ence**, the quality-state suffix. So candence is the candor-state done rightly: it recovers *candidus*'s warmth from the drift that left modern *candor* meaning a coldness-of-honesty the Latin never carried. A person in candence sees the other precisely — no euphemism, no softening by politeness — and engages with care, the clarity and the warmth being one quality, not two in tension.

candence is Asha's attentional register: the mode under which **dokimance** is rightly performed, where validating a truth-claim must be both sharp (to detect verisleight) and careful (the bearer is often vulnerable). Its light is that it lets the Kingdom judge without cruelty and care without dishonesty; its shadow is the counterfeit — bluntness calling itself candor, or blur calling itself care, each pole posing as the whole. Forged in the cathedral at weighted **7.75/10**.
